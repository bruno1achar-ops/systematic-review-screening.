ReviewScreen

Systematic review title and abstract screening · Free · Browser-based

Important: Screening is automated using extracted keywords and rules, not AI. Decisions are preliminary. A protocol PDF may be imperfectly interpreted; verify the criteria and all exclusion decisions before using results in a review.

Import records

      
Upload one or more citation files. Supported formats: CSV and RIS. The app attempts to identify common title, abstract, author, year, DOI and identifier columns.
