How matching works: Explicit exclusion indicators trigger an Exclude recommendation. If required concepts are missing, the record is marked Maybe rather than automatically discarded. If all entered required concept groups match, the recommendation is Include. Comparator and outcome keywords are treated as supporting evidence, not hard requirements, to reduce inappropriate exclusions.
